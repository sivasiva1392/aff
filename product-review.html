Sony WH-1000XM6 Wireless Headphones Review

Industry-leading noise cancellation meets exceptional sound quality. The WH-1000XM6 sets a new benchmark for wireless headphones in 2026.

Expert Review

The Sony WH-1000XM6 continues Sony's dominance in the premium headphone market. With upgraded noise cancellation processors and improved drivers, these headphones deliver an immersive listening experience that rivals much more expensive audiophile gear.

Sound Quality

The 40mm drivers have been retuned for 2026, offering a more balanced sound signature. Bass is punchy without overpowering, mids are clear and detailed, and highs sparkle without sibilance. LDAC and aptX Adaptive support ensures high-resolution audio over Bluetooth.

Noise Cancellation

The new V3 QN1 processor delivers class-leading ANC. Whether you are on a plane, in a busy cafe, or working from home, the WH-1000XM6 creates a peaceful bubble of silence. Ambient sound mode is also improved, letting in just the right amount of external noise.

Battery & Comfort

With 40 hours of playback and quick charge (3 min = 3 hours), battery anxiety is a thing of the past. The redesigned ear cups use memory foam wrapped in soft protein leather for all-day comfort.
