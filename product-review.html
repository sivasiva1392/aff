Primebook 2 Pro (2026) Review

Expert Review

The Primebook 2 Pro (2026) brings the full Android ecosystem to a laptop-optimised form factor. Running Android 15-based PrimeOS 3.0, it offers a true desktop experience with multi-window multitasking, a desktop-style file manager, browser, and smooth keyboard + trackpad input. The MediaTek Helio G99 octa-core processor up to 2.2 GHz delivers smooth multitasking and fast app launches.

Performance & RAM

With 8GB LPDDR4X RAM and 128GB UFS storage, the Primebook 2 Pro handles multiple apps with ease. Storage can be expanded up to 1TB via microSD card, eliminating space anxiety for students and professionals.

Display & Design

The 14.1-inch Full HD IPS anti-glare display delivers crisp visuals, vibrant colours and wide viewing angles. Ideal for multitasking, streaming, presentations and creative work. The backlit keyboard ensures comfortable typing in any lighting condition.

Battery & Connectivity

The 60.3Wh battery delivers up to 14 hours of backup, powering through a full workday. Dual USB Type-A ports, USB Type-C for data and fast charging, a 3.5mm audio jack and a dedicated microSD slot keep you connected. Dual stereo speakers and dual microphones ensure immersive sound and clear voice capture.

Pros

  • Android 15 with laptop-optimised PrimeOS 3.0
  • Up to 14 hours battery life
  • 8GB RAM + 128GB UFS with microSD expansion
  • 14.1-inch FHD IPS anti-glare display
  • Backlit keyboard and in-built AI

Cons

  • No Windows or macOS support
  • Limited to Android app ecosystem
  • No dedicated GPU for heavy gaming
  • Gray colour only
